# Session Workflow

---

## Objective
Every session must have a clear purpose.

## Workflow
1. Define the goal
2. Choose the right phase
3. Apply the relevant checklist
4. Work in focused blocks
5. Save progress and capture learnings

## Phase Mapping
- Foundation: build the core groove and arrangement skeleton
- Sound design: create or refine specific layers
- Arrangement: shape energy and transitions
- Mixing: balance and clarity
- Mastering: final polish and loudness
- Export: delivery and versioning
