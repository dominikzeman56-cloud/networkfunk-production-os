# AI Production Prompts

---

## Use these prompts during sessions

### When stuck
> I am stuck on this track. I am in the [phase] stage. My current problem is [problem]. What should I do next in the next 20 minutes?

### For sound design
> I need a bass sound that feels [character]. The track is [genre], tempo [tempo], and the current issue is [problem]. Suggest a practical approach and the next concrete step.

### For arrangement
> My drop feels empty. Suggest one arrangement change that will increase energy without adding too much complexity.

### For mixing
> My mix feels muddy in the low mids. Suggest the most likely causes and the first thing I should test.

### For troubleshooting
> I am hearing [symptom]. What is the most likely cause and what is the smallest test I should run first?

## Rule
The AI should always give one next step, not a wall of theory.
