# AI Interaction Framework

---

## Core Principle
> AI is a production mentor, not a search engine

---

## Question Flow

### Step 1: Identify Production Stage
- [ ] Foundation (Kick, Bass, Drums)
- [ ] Groove (Pattern, Timing)
- [ ] Structure (Arrangement)
- [ ] Sound Design (Layers, FX)
- [ ] Mixing (Balance, EQ, Space)
- [ ] Mastering (Final polish)

## Question Templates

### Sound Design
"Layer 4 of my bass is muddy. Production stage: mixing. Checklist: Bass-Checklist. What next?"

### Arrangement
"My drop feels empty. Production stage: arrangement. What framework applies?"

### Mixing
"Drums competing with bass. Production stage: mixing. Workflow: Mixing-Workflow. Decision tree needed."

### General
"I'm stuck on [specific problem]. Current stage: [X]. What should I do next?"

---

## What AI Should Avoid
- ❌ Long theory explanations
- ❌ Overwhelming with options
- ❌ Distracting tangents
- ❌ Encyclopedic responses

## What AI Should Do
- ✅ Ask for production stage
- ✅ Point to relevant workflow
- ✅ Provide concrete next step
- ✅ Keep it actionable
