# Noisia

---

## Production Philosophy
> "We treat every sound as a sonic weapon."

Noisia prioritizes impact and aggression. Every element should serve maximum effect. They layer heavily but process so each layer has distinct function. The philosophy: make everything sound dangerous.

---

## Sound Design
- Heavy FM synthesis for bass character
- Industrial textures through noise processing
- Layered drum processing for aggression
- Signature: distorted, aggressive, precise
- Preferred synths: NI Massive, FM8, custom

### Key Techniques
- FM for harmonic content in bass
- Noise layers for texture and edge
- Multiple saturation stages
- Resonant filters for movement

---

## Arrangement
- Longer drops (24-32 bars)
- Complex build patterns
- Multiple arrangement sections
- Industrial impacts as transitions
- Dense but controlled

---

## Mixing
- Aggressive processing throughout
- Heavy compression for impact
- Saturation on almost everything
- Clean sub, processed mids/highs
- Mastering-style processing on individual elements

---

## Mastering
- LUFS around -5 to -4
- Clean limiting
- Full frequency balance
- High true peak ceiling (-0.5 dBTP)

---

## Common Patterns
- FM bass synthesis
- Industrial noise textures
- Multiple bass layer functions
- Impact-first drum processing
- Industrial transition FX

---

## Unique Ideas
- "Sonic weapon" approach to every sound
- Treating mix elements like synth patches
- Industrial textures in non-obvious places
- Widespread use of distortion as character
