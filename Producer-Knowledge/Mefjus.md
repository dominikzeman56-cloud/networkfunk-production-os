# Mefjus

---

## Production Philosophy
> "Clean foundation, organic movement."

Mefjus focuses on clean, warm low end with organic modulation. Sounds are designed to feel natural even when heavily processed. The philosophy: clarity first, character second.

---

## Sound Design
- Clean sub sine waves
- Warm body layers (saw, square)
- Modulation as primary interest
- Organic textures through processing
- Preferred synths: Serum, Phase Plant, Sylenth

### Key Techniques
- Clean sub + processed body
- Filter sweeps for movement
- Organic modulation patterns
- Warm saturation

---

## Arrangement
- Standard Neurofunk structure
- Simpler than Noisia, more grooving
- Transitions through filtering, not FX
- Breakdown space for atmosphere

---

## Mixing
- Warm signal chain
- Gentle compression
- Saturation for character
- Clear low end priority
- Less aggressive than contemporaries

---

## Mastering
- LUFS around -6
- Warm master chain
- Balanced frequencies
- Clean limiting

---

## Common Patterns
- Clean sub sine layer
- Warm body layers with modulation
- Filter automation for movement
- Drum programming over sampling
- Minimal but effective FX

---

## Unique Ideas
- "Organic" movement in bass
- Warmth over aggression
- Filter sweeps as primary interest
- Drum programming complexity

---
