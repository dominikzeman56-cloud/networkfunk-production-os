## Analysis

### Bass
- 3-4 core layers
- Sub (sine), Body (saw/FM), Texture (noise/processed)
- Resonant filter sweeps for movement
- Wobble-type movement controlled

### Drums
- Punchy but not aggressive kick
- Tight snare with layering
- Complex hi-hat patterns
- Percussion accents

### Stereo
- Bass mostly mono
- FX and pads wide
- Good mono compatibility

### Dynamics
- Controlled compression
- Impact from transients
- Breakdown: less compression, more space

### FX
- Minimal but effective
- Filter sweeps prominent
- Space created through reverb tails

### Mix
- Warm low end
- Clear mids
- Present highs without harshness

### Master
- Balanced frequency
- LUFS approx -6

---

## Production Decisions
- Clean sub, processed body
- Filter movement as primary interest
- Drum programming over drum sampling
- Warm saturation throughout

---

## Workflow to Recreate
1. Design clean sub sine layer
2. Create body layer with FM/modulation
3. Add texture layer (processed noise)
4. Route all bass to bus, compress together
5. Design drums with punch priority
6. Add filter automation for movement
7. Keep FX minimal and effective

---

## Lessons Learned
- Clean sub enables heavy body processing
- Filter movement creates interest without adding elements
- Drum programming can be more impactful than sampling
- Warmth from saturation, not softening
