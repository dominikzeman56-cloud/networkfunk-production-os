## Analysis

### Bass
- 4-5 layers identified
- Layer functions: sub, body, modulation, noise, texture
- Heavy FM synthesis in body layer
- Sub stays pure, locked to kick
- Movement: LFO-filter with envelope shaping

### Drums
- Punchy, compressed kick
- Snare with heavy layering and reverb
- Hi-hats programmed with variation
- Tight timing, minimal swing

### Stereo
- Kick/snare/bass: center
- Atmosphere: sides wide
- Processed mid-side for space

### Dynamics
- Heavy compression throughout
- Dynamic range preserved at drops
- Impact moments: kick-snare hits

### FX
- White noise risers
- Industrial impact FX
- Reverb on snare, atmos

### Mix
- Clear low end priority
- Highs enhanced with saturation
- Aggressive but controlled

### Master

- Clean limiting
- Full frequency balance

## Production Decisions
- Sub locked to kick pitch relationship
- FM bass for character, not complexity
- Heavy compression for impact
- Industrial FX for signature Noisia sound

---

## Workflow to Recreate
1. Start with kick, design pure sub layer
2. Add FM bass layer with modulation
3. Layer modulation on bass
4. Add noise/texture layer
5. Compress all bass elements together
6. Process drums for punch
7. Build arrangement around bass-drum relationship

---

## Lessons Learned
- Sub clarity > complexity
- Bass processing as single unit, not layers
- Industrial FX add character without clutter
- Compression glue creates cohesion
