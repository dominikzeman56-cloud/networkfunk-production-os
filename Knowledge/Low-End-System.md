# Low End System

---

## Core Principle
Low end should be controlled, intentional, and useful in the mix.

## Priority Order
1. Kick definition
2. Bass clarity
3. Sub support
4. Harmonic body
5. Stereo and movement

## Common Structure
- Sub layer: fundamental support
- Body layer: body and weight
- Movement layer: modulation or sidechain movement
- Texture layer: grit or air if needed

## Checks
- [ ] Kick and bass occupy different space
- [ ] Sub is mono and controlled
- [ ] Mid-bass does not mask the kick
- [ ] Low end survives translation
- [ ] No unnecessary rumble

## Typical Problems
- Weak sub
- Muddy low mids
- Kick-bass clash
- Phase issues
- Overly wide low end
