# Humanization

---

## Core Principle
> Perfect quantize kills groove. Add variation intentionally.

---

## Quantize vs Humanize

### Quantize
- Locks to grid
- Perfect timing
- No variation
- Use for: tight rhythms, techno

### Humanize
- Adds variation
- Natural feel
- Controlled randomness
- Use for: organic feel, groovy sections

---

## Humanization Techniques

### Velocity Variation
- Range: ±5-15%
- More on hats, less on kick/snare
- Subtle variation, not random
- Keep dynamics musical

### Timing Variation
- Swing: 5-20%
- Random: ±10-30ms
- Apply selectively
- Keep kick/snare tight

### Pitch Variation
- Detune within key
- Subtle pitch drift
- More on long notes
- Less on percussive

### Modulation Variation
- Slight LFO rate changes
- Envelope variations
- Filter cutoff drift
- Keep subtle

---

## Application by Element

### Kick
- Keep tight
- Velocity: ±3%
- Timing: locked

### Snare
- Keep tight
- Velocity: ±5%
- Timing: ±10ms

### Hi-Hats
- Velocity: ±10-15%
- Timing: ±10-20ms
- Swing: 10-15%

### Bass
- Velocity: ±5%
- Modulation: slight variation
- Keep timing relatively tight

### Pads
- Velocity: ±5%
- Pitch: slight detuning
- More humanization

### Leads
- Velocity: ±10%
- Timing: ±10-20ms
- Expression variation

---

## Tips

1. Start with subtle variations
2. More variation = more organic
3. Keep kick and snare on grid
4. Hats and leads get more variation
5. Velocity affects groove more than timing
6. Test in mono for mono issues
7. Compare with and without humanization
