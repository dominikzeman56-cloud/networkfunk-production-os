# Mixing System

---

## Core Principle
Mixing is not a separate stage. It begins during sound design and arrangement.

## Order of Operations
1. Balance foundation elements
2. Clean problem frequencies
3. Add movement and space
4. Check mono compatibility
5. Compare to reference
6. Make final decisions with restraint

## Focus Areas
- Kick and bass relationship
- Drum clarity
- Stereo placement
- Dynamic movement
- Headroom and loudness

## Checklist
- [ ] Low end is controlled
- [ ] Drums are clear
- [ ] Bass has space
- [ ] FX support the track
- [ ] Mix translates to headphones and speakers
- [ ] Mastering headroom remains intact
