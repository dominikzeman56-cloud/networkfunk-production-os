# Bass Engineering

---

## Core Principle
Bass should be felt before it is analyzed. The goal is clarity, weight, and movement.

## Bass Architecture
- Sub: fundamental support
- Body: harmonic weight
- Movement: modulation and shaping
- Texture: grit or edge

## Common Workflow
1. Build the sub first
2. Add body for weight
3. Add movement for character
4. Test in the full mix
5. Carve space from competing frequencies

## Checks
- [ ] Sub is clear and mono compatible
- [ ] Kick and bass are not fighting
- [ ] Mid-bass is purposeful
- [ ] Movement is controlled
- [ ] The bass still works in the mix after processing

## Typical Problems
- Muddy low mids
- Weak sub
- Kick-bass masking
- Overly wide bass
- No movement
