# Dynamics

---

## Core Principle
> Control dynamics, don't eliminate them

---

## Dynamic Range

### Loud (Neurofunk drop)
- Heavy compression
- 6-10 dB dynamic range
- Consistent energy

### Controlled (Breakdown)
- Lighter compression
- 12-18 dB dynamic range
- Natural feel

### Dynamic (Intro/Outro)
- Gentle compression
- 15-20 dB dynamic range
- More variation

---

## Compression Guidelines

### Kick
- Ratio: 4:1 - 8:1
- Attack: 0.1-10 ms (fast)
- Release: 50-150 ms
- Purpose: punch, consistency

### Bass
- Ratio: 2:1 - 4:1
- Attack: 5-20 ms
- Release: 100-300 ms
- Purpose: glue, control

### Snare
- Ratio: 4:1 - 8:1
- Attack: 0.1-5 ms (fast)
- Release: 50-150 ms
- Purpose: snap, body

### Bus/Drum Group
- Ratio: 2:1 - 4:1
- Attack: 5-15 ms
- Release: 150-300 ms
- Purpose: glue

### Master
- Ratio: 2:1 - 4:1
- Attack: 5-10 ms
- Release: Auto or 200ms+
- Purpose: consistency

---

## Parallel Compression

### Kick
- Dry: 50-70%
- Compressed: 30-50%
- Purpose: punch + character

### Snare
- Dry: 40-60%
- Compressed: 40-60%
- Purpose: crack + body

### Bass
- Dry: 60-80%
- Compressed: 20-40%
- Purpose: punch + weight

---

## Dynamic Contrast

- Quiet before loud = impact
- Drops louder than builds = payoff
- Breakdown less compressed = space
