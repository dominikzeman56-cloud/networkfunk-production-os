# Drum Engineering

---

## Core Principle
Drums should create impact, groove, and clarity without wasting space.

## Kick
- Define the body and transient separately
- Keep the fundamental clear
- Avoid too much low-mid clutter

## Snare
- Make the transient readable
- Control decay if needed
- Use space around the snare for clarity

## Hats and Percussion
- Keep them rhythmic and controlled
- Avoid too much high-end smear
- Use variation to support energy flow

## Workflow
1. Build the kick first
2. Add snare and hats
3. Balance the drum family
4. Compare to reference
5. Make the groove feel alive
