# Arrangement

---

## Core Principle
> Energy flows, it doesn't switch

---

## Structure Templates

### Standard Neurofunk
```
Intro (8-16 bars)
    ↓
Build (8-16 bars)
    ↓
Drop A (16-32 bars)
    ↓
Breakdown (8-16 bars)
    ↓
Drop B (16-32 bars)
    ↓
Outro (4-8 bars)
```

### Extended Structure
```
Intro (8 bars)
Build (8 bars)
Drop A (16 bars)
Breakdown 1 (8 bars)
Drop B (16 bars)
Build 2 (8 bars)
Drop C (16 bars)
Breakdown 2 (8 bars)
Drop D (16 bars)
Outro (8 bars)
```

---

## Energy Flow Rules

- Builds should build **up**, not just **out**
- Drop energy > build energy (payoff)
- Breakdowns need melodic/atmospheric content
- Sections should contrast, not repeat identically
- Use filter automation to shape energy

---

## Section Guidelines

### Intro
- Establish mood
- Introduce melodic motif
- Set tempo/key reference
- Keep energy controlled

### Build
- Add elements progressively
- Use risers and tension
- Remove low end
- Increase rhythm density

### Drop
- Maximum energy
- Full bass + drums
- Clear impact moment
- Groove locked in

### Breakdown
- Space to breathe
- Atmospheric content
- Reverb tails
- Set up next drop

### Outro
- Energy reduction
- Leave satisfying ending
- Not abrupt cut

## Transition Types

- Filter sweep
- White noise riser
- Bass ramp
- Drum fill
- Reverse cymbal
- Vocal sample
- Automation ride
