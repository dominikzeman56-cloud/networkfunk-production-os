# Arrangement Principles

---

## Core Principle
Arrangement is not decoration. It is the system that creates tension, release, and momentum.

## Section Design
- Intro: establish mood and space
- Build: add energy gradually
- Drop: define the core impact
- Breakdown: create contrast
- Outro: resolve or leave space

## Energy Rules
- Each section should have a clear role
- Avoid static sections for too long
- Use automation and filtering to support movement
- Change the texture, not just the volume

## Practical Checklist
- [ ] Each section has a clear purpose
- [ ] The energy curve feels intentional
- [ ] There is contrast between sections
- [ ] Transitions are clear and musical
- [ ] The track does not overstay its welcome
