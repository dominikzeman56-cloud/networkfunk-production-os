# Reverb

---

## Core Principle
> Space creates depth. Too much space creates mud.

---

## Reverb Types

### Room
- Small, tight
- Short decay (0.5-1.5s)
- Best for: drums, percussion
- Character: natural, tight

### Hall
- Medium, spacious
- Medium decay (1.5-3s)
- Best for: snare, atmosphere
- Character: warm, natural

### Plate
- Dense, smooth
- Medium decay (1-3s)
- Best for: snare, vocals
- Character: smooth, dense

### Spring
- Bouncy, character
- Variable decay
- Best for: experimental, FX
- Character: vintage, warm

### Shimmer
- Pitch-shifted reverb
- Long decay (3-8s)
- Best for: atmosphere, builds
- Character: ethereal, wide

---

## Reverb Parameters

### Decay Time
- Short (0.3-1s): drums, tight space
- Medium (1-3s): snare, general
- Long (3-8s): atmosphere, breakdowns

### Pre-Delay
- 0-20ms: tight, natural
- 20-50ms: space before reverb
- 50ms+: clear separation

### Size
- Small: room, drum reverb
- Medium: hall, snare reverb
- Large: cathedral, atmosphere

### Diffusion
- Low: distinct reflections
- High: smooth, dense

---

## Common Uses

### Kick
- Short room or plate
- Decay: 0.3-0.8s
- Pre-delay: 0-10ms
- Mix: 5-15%

### Snare
- Medium hall or plate
- Decay: 1-2s
- Pre-delay: 0-20ms
- Mix: 15-30%

### Hi-Hats
- Short room
- Decay: 0.3-0.5s
- Pre-delay: 0ms
- Mix: 5-10%

### Atmosphere/Pads
- Long hall or shimmer
- Decay: 3-6s
- Pre-delay: 20-50ms
- Mix: 25-50%

### Send vs Insert
- Send: shared reverb space
- Insert: character verb on specific element

---

## Tips

1. High-pass before reverb (except bass if intended)
2. Pre-delay separates dry from wet
3. Check in mono for mono compatibility
4. Use reverb for depth, not decoration
5. Less is usually more
6. Sidechain reverb for clarity
7. EQ reverb if competing with dry signal
