## Layer-Specific FX

### Kick FX
- Transient shaper
- Saturation
- Compression (punch)
- Limiter

### Bass FX
- Saturation
- Distortion
- Filter
- Chorus (for stereo)

### Snare FX
- Reverb
- Saturation
- Compression
- Gater (if needed)

### Atmosphere FX
- Reverb (long tails)
- Delay (subtle)
- Filtering

---

## Signal Chain Best Practices

```
Source → EQ → Saturation → Modulation → Compression → Reverb/Delay → Output
```

- EQ first (clean up)
- Saturation early (harmonics)
- Modulation after saturation
- Compression after modulation
- Space last (wet processing)
