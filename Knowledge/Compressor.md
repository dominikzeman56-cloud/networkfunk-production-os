# Compressor

---

## Core Principle
> Control dynamics, don't eliminate them. Compression adds character, not just reduction.

---

## Compressor Types

### VCA
- Clean, fast
- Best for: precision control
- Character: transparent, surgical

### Opto
- Smooth, musical
- Best for: vocals, mastering
- Character: warm, natural

### FET
- Fast, aggressive
- Best for: drums, parallel
- Character: punchy, colored

### Variable Mu
- Smooth, natural
- Best for: mastering, glue
- Character: warm, musical

---

## Parameters

### Threshold
- Higher: more compression
- Lower: less compression
- Set by ear for character

### Ratio
- Low (1.5:1-2:1): gentle control
- Medium (3:1-5:1): standard
- High (8:1+): limiting, extreme

### Attack
- Fast (0.01-10 ms): punch, transient control
- Medium (10-30 ms): balanced
- Slow (30-100 ms): preserve transients

### Release
- Fast (50-150 ms): pumping feel
- Medium (150-300 ms): natural
- Slow (300+ ms): preserves dynamics

### Makeup Gain
- Compensate for reduction
- Match input/output levels
- Gain staging important

---

## Common Settings

### Kick
- VCA or FET
- Ratio: 4:1-8:1
- Attack: 0.1-5 ms
- Release: 50-150 ms
- Purpose: punch, consistency

### Snare
- FET or Opto
- Ratio: 4:1-8:1
- Attack: 0.1-10 ms
- Release: 50-150 ms
- Purpose: snap, body

### Bass
- VCA or Opto
- Ratio: 2:1-4:1
- Attack: 5-20 ms
- Release: 100-300 ms
- Purpose: control, glue

### Drums Bus
- Opto or Variable Mu
- Ratio: 2:1-4:1
- Attack: 10-20 ms
- Release: 150-300 ms
- Purpose: glue, consistency

### Master Bus
- Opto or Variable Mu
- Ratio: 1.5:1-2:1
- Attack: 5-10 ms
- Release: auto or 200ms+
- Purpose: glue, consistency

---

## Parallel Compression

### Formula
- Dry signal: 50-70%
- Compressed signal: 30-50%
- Blend to taste

### Best For
- Adding punch without over-compression
- Maintaining transients
- Aggressive sounds

---

## Tips

1. Listen for character, not just reduction
2. Attack affects punch, release affects pump
3. Higher ratio with slower attack = smoother
4. Lower ratio with faster attack = more audible
5. Parallel compression adds character safely
6. Sidechain for clarity, not eliminating dynamics
7. Don't over-compress - dynamic range is valuable
