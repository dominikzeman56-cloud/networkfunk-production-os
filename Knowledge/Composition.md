# Composition

---

## Core Principle
> Energy flows through structure. Structure serves energy.

---

## Musical Elements

### Rhythm
- 4/4 time signature standard
- 16th note foundation
- Syncopation as accent
- Groove over quantized perfection

### Harmony
- Minor keys common (Dm, Am, Em)
- Modal interchange for color
- Drone notes for anchor
- Sparse chord usage

### Melody
- Short motifs
- Rhythmic variation
- Call and response
- Development through mutation

---

## Neurofunk Patterns

### Bass Motif
- 2-4 bar pattern
- Repetitive but evolving
- Syncopated rhythm
- Pitch variation within key

### Drum Pattern
- Kick on 1 and 3
- Snare on 2 and 4
- 16th note hats with variation
- Off-beat accents

### Chord Progression
- Simple progressions
- Minor keys
- Modal borrowing
- Drone chord

---

## Motif Development

### Variation Techniques
- Rhythm change
- Pitch transposition
- Timbre change
- Addition/subtraction
- Reversal

### Development Process
1. Establish motif (4-8 bars)
2. First variation (add interest)
3. Second variation (change character)
4. Development (break down, build up)
5. Resolution (return, transformed)

## Rhythmic Structure

### Standard Pattern
```
Kick:    1 . . . 2 . . . 3 . . . 4 . . .
Snare:   . . . . 1 . . . . . . . 1 . . .
Hats:    1 e + a 2 e + a 3 e + a 4 e + a
Bass:    1 - - 2 - 3 - - - - 4 - -
```

### Syncopated Pattern
- Emphasize off-beats
- Delayed bass hits
- Anticipated snare
- 16th note variation

## Harmonic Movement

### Common Progressions (in Dm)
- Dm → Bb → Am → G
- Dm → F → C → Bb
- Dm → Am → Bb → F

### Modal Interchange
- Borrow from Phrygian
- Borrow from Dorian
- Lydian for brightness

### Drone Technique
- Static root note
- Movement above
- Tension through upper harmony
